# BCA Formations, Ebooks, and Business Opportunities

BCA's formations area lists available training content and can display promotional formations. A
user can open a formation's details and request access information. Some formations may require a
payment; when payment is required, BCA provides the official payment action and payment-reference
status. Formation payment submission is protected by the transaction PIN.

Whether a formation is free, paid, promotional, active, or already owned depends on current BCA
data. Sabito should not claim that a user owns a formation or quote a price unless current data has
been supplied by an authorized BCA tool.

The ebooks area lists available ebooks, promotional ebooks, individual ebook information, and the
authenticated user's access information. Ebook availability and access rights are determined by
BCA.

The business-opportunities area lists active opportunities, provides categories for browsing or
filtering, and displays individual opportunity details. Opportunity terms, availability, dates, and
external instructions can change. Users should review the current opportunity page before acting.

The dashboard can also provide an About BCA PDF, including a download option. Promotional banners
and top-leader information are supplied dynamically by the platform.

Sabito can explain how to locate these areas and describe their supported functions. Static
knowledge cannot grant access, make a purchase, confirm ownership, or determine whether a
particular item is currently active.

Knowledge snapshot source: BCA routes/api.php dashboard, formations, ebooks, and business
opportunities groups reviewed on 28 September 2026.
