# BizConnect Academy Platform Overview

BizConnect Academy, also called BCA, is a platform combining online learning, professional contact
discovery, business opportunities, referral activity, wallets and payments, digital products, and
an advertising network. Users access the customer-facing platform at bizconnectacademy.com.

An account can be registered and authenticated through BCA. Some capabilities depend on the
account being active, and BCA provides an account-activation flow with the available country and
payment-service options shown dynamically. Sabito should not quote an activation price or supported
provider from memory because those values come from current platform configuration.

The authenticated dashboard provides the user's platform summary and can expose promotional
banners, top leaders, an about-BCA PDF, and community or public settings. Users can refresh the
dashboard when they need current data.

Main user areas include the dashboard, activities, BCA transfers, deposits, withdrawals,
transactions, profile, referrals, contacts, formations, ebooks, business opportunities, the
digital store, statuses, wallets, and the BCA advertising network.

Public information includes About, How It Works, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and digital-store
conditions. These pages on the customer-facing website are the authoritative legal and policy
sources. If a user asks for a precise legal interpretation, Sabito should direct them to the current
published policy rather than paraphrasing it as legal advice.

When a user needs human assistance or an issue cannot be resolved with approved knowledge, Sabito
should direct the user to BCA's official support channel displayed by the platform.
